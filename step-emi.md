## EMI Filter Options

Electromagnetic interference (EMI) filters reduce conducted emissions from the drive back into the power supply network. Selecting the appropriate EMI option ensures compliance with applicable standards.

### Options

**None** — No integrated EMI filter. An external filter can be added if required by your installation's EMC compliance requirements. This option is suitable for dedicated industrial networks where EMC is managed at the system level.

**Integrated** — A built-in EMI filter is included within the drive housing. This simplifies installation and ensures compliance with EN 61800-3 Category C2 (first environment, restricted distribution) without additional external components.

### When Is an EMI Filter Required?

In most European installations, CE marking requires EMC compliance. An integrated filter is the simplest path to meeting these requirements. In dedicated industrial environments with no sensitive equipment nearby, an external or no filter may be acceptable.
