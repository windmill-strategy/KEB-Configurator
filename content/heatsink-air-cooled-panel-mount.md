-e ## Air Cooled — Panel Mount

Standard forced-air cooling with the drive mounted fully inside an electrical cabinet. All heat losses are dissipated within the enclosure.

### Thermal Characteristics

The drive's internal fan draws ambient air from within the cabinet and exhausts it through the heatsink fins. Cabinet ventilation or climate control must account for the drive's total power dissipation.

### When to Choose

The most common configuration for standard industrial cabinets. Simplest to install and maintain, suitable for environments with ambient temperatures up to 40°C without derating.
