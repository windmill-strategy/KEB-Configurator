## Selecting Your Input Voltage

The input voltage is determined by the power supply available at your installation site. Use the IEC/UL toggle to match your regional standard.

### IEC vs UL

**IEC** voltages follow International Electrotechnical Commission standards, used across Europe, Asia, and most global markets. Common options include 230V single-phase, 230V three-phase, and 400V three-phase.

**UL** voltages follow Underwriters Laboratories standards, primarily used in North America. The three-phase high-voltage option is rated at 480V rather than 400V.

### Things to Consider

Ensure your facility's electrical infrastructure matches the voltage and standard you select. Mismatched voltage ratings can result in equipment damage or reduced performance.
