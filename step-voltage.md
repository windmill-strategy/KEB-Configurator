## Selecting Your Input Voltage

The input voltage is determined by the power supply available at your installation site. This is typically the first and most critical decision in sizing your drive.

### Common Configurations

**230V 3-Phase** is the standard industrial supply for smaller motor applications. It is widely available in most manufacturing environments and suitable for drives up to medium power ranges.

**400V 3-Phase** is the most common configuration for industrial applications in Europe and many global markets. It supports the full range of power ratings and is recommended for applications above 30 kW.

**230V 1-Phase** is available for lower power applications where three-phase supply is not accessible. This is common in light commercial or workshop environments.

### Things to Consider

Ensure your facility's electrical infrastructure matches the voltage you select. Mismatched voltage ratings can result in equipment damage or reduced performance. Consult your facility's electrical documentation or a qualified electrician if you are unsure of your supply voltage.
