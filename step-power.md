## Choosing the Right Power Rating

Select the power rating that matches your motor's nameplate specifications. You can search by kilowatts (KW) or horsepower (HP) depending on your region and preference.

### How to Determine Power Requirements

Check the nameplate on your motor for its rated power output. The drive's power rating should match or slightly exceed the motor's rated power to ensure reliable operation under all load conditions.

### KW vs HP

**Kilowatts (KW)** are the standard unit of measure in IEC (International Electrotechnical Commission) regions, including Europe and most of Asia.

**Horsepower (HP)** is the standard unit of measure in NEMA (National Electrical Manufacturers Association) regions, primarily North America.

### Derating Considerations

At higher ambient temperatures or altitudes above 1000m, you may need to select a higher power rating to compensate for thermal derating. Consult the product technical manual for detailed derating curves.
