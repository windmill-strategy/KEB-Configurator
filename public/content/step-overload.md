## Selecting the Overload Characteristic

The rated switching frequency determines the drive's overload capability and output current waveform quality.

### Available Ratings

**2 kHz** — Highest overload capacity. Best for heavy-duty applications.

**4 kHz** — Standard for most industrial applications. Good balance of waveform quality and overload.

**6 kHz** — Improved motor current quality and reduced audible noise.

**8 kHz** — Smoothest output waveform and lowest motor noise.

### Trade-off

Higher switching frequencies improve motor efficiency and reduce noise, but increase drive losses and reduce maximum overload current.
