## Selecting EMI Option

Electromagnetic interference (EMI) filters reduce conducted emissions from the drive back into the power supply network.

### Options

**None** — No integrated EMI filter. Suitable for dedicated industrial networks where EMC is managed at the system level.

**Integrated** — A built-in EMI filter simplifies installation and ensures compliance with EN 61800-3 Category C2 without additional external components.

### When Is an EMI Filter Required?

In most European installations, CE marking requires EMC compliance. An integrated filter is the simplest path to meeting these requirements.
