-e ## Air Cooled (Convection) — Push-Through Mount

Passive convection cooling with no internal fan. Silent operation with zero fan maintenance.

### Thermal Characteristics

Natural convection relies on passive airflow over the heatsink fins. Current ratings may be reduced compared to fan-cooled variants.

### When to Choose

Low-noise environments, dusty conditions where fan filters would clog, or where fan maintenance access is difficult.
