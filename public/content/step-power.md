## Selecting the Right Power Rating

Select the power rating that matches your motor's nameplate specifications. You can search by kilowatts (kW) or horsepower (Hp).

### How to Determine Power Requirements

Check the nameplate on your motor for its rated power output. The drive's power rating should match or slightly exceed the motor's rated power.

### kW vs Hp

**Kilowatts (kW)** are the standard unit of measure in IEC regions, including Europe and most of Asia.

**Horsepower (Hp)** is the standard unit in NEMA regions, primarily North America.

### Derating Considerations

At higher ambient temperatures or altitudes above 1000m, you may need to select a higher power rating. Consult the product technical manual for derating curves.
