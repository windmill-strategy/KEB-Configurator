## Selecting the Drive Type

The drive type determines the core topology, form factor, and feature set.

### F6 — Wallmount

The standard frequency inverter for general-purpose industrial applications. The wallmount form factor is designed for standalone cabinet installations. Ideal for fans, pumps, conveyors, compressors, and other general industrial loads.

### S6 — Rackmount

The servo-grade variant designed for high-dynamic applications requiring precise positioning, speed regulation, and torque control. The rackmount form factor is optimized for multi-axis cabinet installations. Ideal for CNC machines, robotics, packaging machinery, and high-precision applications.
